Questions

Before you submit your project.

Do I need drawings or exact measurements?

No. Give us the information you have. More detailed measurements and selections can be handled later if the project moves forward.

What areas do you serve?

Scorva Projects is focused on Tampa Bay and surrounding communities. Submit your project ZIP code so we can review location and fit.

Does submitting the form commit me to a project?

No. A project request is simply the first step so your needs can be reviewed and the appropriate next conversation can happen.

What types of projects can I submit?

Kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, flooring, interior or exterior painting, and multi-room or whole-home improvement projects.
